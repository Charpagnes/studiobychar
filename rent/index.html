Rent questions

What is included in the studio rental?

The Concept comes with about 100 sqm of natural light space, several ready-made sets (sofa, kitchenette, arch, velvet, cane chair), a backdrop system, continuous lighting and props. The Set comes with podcast and video sets, cameras, microphones and sound-treated walls. A technician is on site.

Can I rent The Concept and The Set separately?

Yes. The Concept (downstairs) and The Set (upstairs) are separate rentals, so you can book either one or both.

What is the minimum rental?

Studio rental is a minimum of 4 hours. The Concept can also be booked in the evening, from 5pm to 9pm.

Can I bring my own lighting or photographer?

Yes to both. Our lighting is there if you want it, and you are welcome to bring your own gear and your own photographer.

Is the studio good for fashion and product shoots?

Yes. Natural light, high ceilings and several sets make The Concept a good fit for fashion, product, brand and lifestyle work. Want us to run the shoot too? Have a look at Hire.

How do I book and pay?

Pick a slot online and pay in full upfront to lock it in. Not sure which space fits? Book a 20-minute call.
