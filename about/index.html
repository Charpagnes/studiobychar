The vision behind the space

Space shapes content. A bad space gives you mediocre work, however much skill walks through the door.

Charlotte spent years shooting on location across Southeast Asia, watching talented creatives wrestle with poor spaces, broken kit and no creative support. Studio by Char is the fix.

Every set was designed the way Charlotte wants to shoot, and every piece of kit was chosen the way she wants to work. It feels less like a rental and more like a collaborator.
