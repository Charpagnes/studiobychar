Hire questions

What is the difference between booking, a call and a quote?

Headshots and comp cards you can book and pay for online. Social media content starts with a 20-minute discovery call. Product, fashion and production projects start with a short brief so we can send you a quote.

Can I rent the studio and bring my own photographer?

Yes. Studio rental and our production services are separate, so you can hire the space on its own.

What do you need for a quote?

Your brand, what you sell, roughly how many shots you need, where the images will be used, your dates, a budget range and any references you love.

How are the files delivered?

Edited, retouched and delivered through a private gallery, ready to publish.
